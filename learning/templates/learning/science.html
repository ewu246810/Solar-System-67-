A FEW USEFUL STARTING POINTS

Key science ideas

01

Observation: something you notice using your senses or a tool, such as a thermometer.

02

Evidence: information you collect that can help answer a question.

03

Prediction: a reasoned idea about what might happen, which you can investigate.

04

Model: a useful way to represent something that may be too large, small, or complex to see all at once.

FROM CURIOSITY TO EVIDENCE

How a science investigation works

ASK · PREDICT · INVESTIGATE · EXPLAIN

Start with a question you can explore. Make a prediction and decide what you will observe or measure. Record what happens, then compare the evidence with your prediction. Your results may support your idea, change it, or lead to a new question. A good investigation is careful and fair, and other people should be able to understand what you did.

QUESTION

Does light affect plant growth?

Ask a question that can be explored by observing plants over time.

FAIR TEST

Change one thing

Compare plants in different light while keeping other conditions as similar as you can.

OBSERVE

Record what happens

Measure or describe the plants regularly. Notes and measurements are evidence.

EXPLAIN

Use the evidence

Describe what your observations suggest, and mention anything that could affect the result.

OUR COSMIC NEIGHBOURHOOD

A quick guide to the solar system

The Sun and the objects held by its gravity make up our solar system.

ONE STAR · EIGHT PLANETS · MANY SMALLER WORLDS

The Sun is a star at the centre of our solar system. In order from the Sun, the eight planets are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. The inner four are rocky planets; Jupiter and Saturn are gas giants, while Uranus and Neptune are ice giants. Planets travel around the Sun in paths called orbits.

THE SUN

A star, not a planet

It gives Earth most of the light and heat that make life here possible.

EARTH

Our home planet

Earth has liquid water on its surface and supports a great variety of life.

THE MOON

Earth's natural satellite

It travels around Earth. A moon is a natural object that orbits a planet.

DWARF PLANETS

Smaller worlds, too

Pluto is a dwarf planet in the Kuiper Belt beyond Neptune.

EXTRA THINGS TO KNOW

Science is all around us

01

Plants use light energy, water, and carbon dioxide to make sugars through photosynthesis.

02

Many materials change when heated or cooled. Water can be a solid, liquid, or gas.

03

Gravity pulls objects toward one another. Earth's gravity keeps us on the ground and the Moon in orbit.

04

Scientific explanations can improve when people collect new evidence and check earlier ideas.
