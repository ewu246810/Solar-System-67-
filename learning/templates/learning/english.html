READING WARM-UPWhat can you infer from this short scene?+

“The rain stopped just before sunset. Maya stepped into the garden and carefully lifted a small blue notebook from beneath the bench.”

Why might Maya lift the notebook carefully?

One possible answer: She may be trying not to damage it. The word “carefully” is a clue about how she handles the notebook.
