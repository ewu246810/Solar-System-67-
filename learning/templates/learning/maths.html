Key maths ideas

A USEFUL WAY TO THINK

How to solve a maths problem

READ · CHOOSE · WORK · CHECK

First, read the question and decide what it is asking. Choose a method that matches the problem, then show each step so you can follow your thinking. At the end, check whether your answer makes sense. If you are finding part of a group, your answer should not be bigger than the whole group.

FRACTIONS

Find ¾ of 20

Split 20 into 4 equal groups: 20 ÷ 4 = 5. Take 3 groups: 5 × 3 = 15.

PERCENTAGES

Find 25% of 80

25% is the same as one quarter. One quarter of 80 is 80 ÷ 4 = 20.

ALGEBRA

Solve x + 7 = 12

Undo adding 7 by subtracting 7 from both sides. Then x = 12 − 7, so x = 5.

GEOMETRY

Find a rectangle’s area

Multiply its length by its width. A 6 cm by 3 cm rectangle has area 6 × 3 = 18 cm².

YOUR TURNWhat is 10% of 70?+

Answer: 7. Ten per cent means 10 out of every 100, or one tenth. One tenth of 70 is 70 ÷ 10 = 7.

NOTICE THE CONNECTIONS

One idea can have different forms

A fraction, decimal, and percentage can describe the same amount.

SAME VALUE, THREE WAYS

½ = 0.5 = 50%. The fraction ½ means one part out of two equal parts. Dividing 1 by 2 gives the decimal 0.5. As a percentage, 0.5 of a whole is 50 out of 100, or 50%.
